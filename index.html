Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Finance at Imperial College London. My research focuses on financial intermediation, insurance markets, and asset pricing.

I hold an MSc in Finance and Economics from the London School of Economics and Political Science and a BSc in Business Administration from LMU Munich. During my PhD, I held research positions at the Deutsche Bundesbank and the European Central Bank. Before joining Imperial, I worked in the DG Macroprudential Policy & Financial Stability of the European Central Bank.
